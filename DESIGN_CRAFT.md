# FEST LIFE — Design Craft Standards (Anti-AI)

1. **Poster Typography & Sentence Case:**
   - Tight display tracking (`-0.03em`) on headings, tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) on KPIs.
   - Use natural Sentence case for labels and microcopy instead of spaced-out `ALL CAPS`.
2. **Surface Hierarchy Over Borders:**
   - Replace nested glowing borders ("cards inside cards") with subtle tonal surface elevation and `1px` hairline dividers.
3. **Unobstructed Artist Imagery:**
   - Never bury artist portraits under heavy dark scrims; use crisp framing, `object-position: center 20%`, and subtle bottom edge legibility only where text overlaps.
4. **Calibrated Motion (`160ms–240ms`):**
   - Animate only `transform` and `opacity` using `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
